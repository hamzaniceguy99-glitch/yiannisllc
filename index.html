How we work

A Florida shop, and we check the artwork

Yiannis LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L09000024109, filed in 2009, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

The rule that matters most here: you must own the artwork you send us, or hold written permission to use it. We decline orders reproducing a third party trade mark, league or team insignia, character or photograph without evidence of authorisation — not because we are difficult, but because the demand letter goes to whoever ordered the shirts. We are also honest about colour: screens, threads and fabrics do not match a monitor, and dye lots vary.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you print this logo I found?

Only if you own it or hold written permission. We will ask, and we will decline otherwise — reproducing someone else's mark exposes you, not just us.

Will the colour match exactly?

Close, not exact. Ink and thread look different from a screen, vary between fabrics and shift between dye lots. If an exact match matters, tell us up front and we will show you a sample first.

Screen print or transfer?

Depends on quantity and colours. Screen printing wins on larger runs and durability; transfer wins on a dozen shirts or full-colour artwork. We will tell you which your order actually suits.

Can I bring my own shirts?

Yes, at your risk. If a piece spoils in production we cannot replace a garment we did not supply, so bring a spare or two.

Why does embroidery cost more the first time?

Artwork has to be digitised into a stitch file. That is a one-off, and we keep the file so reorders are cheaper.

I approved the proof and a name is misspelled.

Then it prints as approved, which is why we ask you to check every name and number carefully. We will always flag anything that looks wrong, but the approved proof governs.

Got artwork and a quantity?

Send both and we will quote the method that actually suits the job.
